Replace the applications list with a board: four columns from drafting to closed, cards with page one of the resume, the page result, when each last moved, the outcome or an offer, and the agent's changes waiting for review. Drag a card, or carry it with the keyboard, to change its status; closing asks how it ended. Moves save in the background with Undo, and a failed save puts the card back. Search with `/`, show archived cards from View, and archive or unarchive from a card's menu.
